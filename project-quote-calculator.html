How it is calculated

Subtotal = sum of (hours x hourly rate).
Revision buffer = subtotal x revision %.
Rush surcharge = (subtotal + revision buffer) x rush %.
Quote total = subtotal + revision buffer + rush surcharge.
Deposit = total x deposit %; the balance is due on delivery.
You keep = total minus the platform percentage and any fixed fee. Withdrawal fees and your own taxes are not included.

Example: 10 hours at $50 and 2 hours at $100 is $700. A 10% revision buffer adds $70 and a 25% rush surcharge adds $192.50, so the quote is $962.50 and a 50% deposit is $481.25.

FAQ

How do I calculate a freelance project quote?

Multiply the hours for each task by your hourly rate, add them up, then add a buffer for revisions and, if the client needs it fast, a rush surcharge. The result is the quote total.

How much revision buffer should I add?

There is no standard number. Many freelancers add 10-20% for work with clear scope and more when the brief is vague. Pick the percentage that matches how you work and state the number of included revisions in your quote.

Does the rush surcharge apply to the revision buffer?

In this calculator, yes: the rush percentage is applied to the items subtotal plus the revision buffer. You can set the surcharge to 0 if you do not charge one.

What does the deposit line show?

It is the chosen percentage of the quote total to request upfront, and the balance is what remains on delivery. Deposit terms are agreed between you and your client.

Are the platform fees accurate?

PayPal 3.49% + $0.49 is verified on PayPal's US page. The Fiverr 20% commission is verified on Fiverr Help Center. The Upwork 10% starting value is unverified; Upwork's fee is 0-15% per contract, so enter the rate on your own proposal. Withdrawal fees are not included.
