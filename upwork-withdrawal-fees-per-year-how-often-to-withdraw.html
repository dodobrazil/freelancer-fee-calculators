Upwork Withdrawal Fees per Year: How Often Should You Withdraw?

Upwork charges a flat fee per withdrawal, not a percentage. That makes how often you withdraw a real lever: the same earnings cost very different amounts depending on the method and the schedule.

FAQ

How often should I withdraw from Upwork?

Flat withdrawal fees are paid per withdrawal, so fewer, larger withdrawals cost less over a year. With the $0.99 local-bank fee the difference between weekly ($51.48) and monthly ($11.88) is small; with a $50 wire it is $2,600.00 versus $600.00.

Is there a minimum or waiting period that changes this?

Withdrawal timing and limits are set on each Upwork method page and can change. This guide only uses the published fee per withdrawal; check the linked help pages for limits.

Does this include the Upwork service fee?

No. The freelancer service fee (0% to 15% per contract) is deducted from each payment before you withdraw and is separate. Your own bank may also add incoming fees or an exchange spread, which are not included.
