PayPal Fees for Receiving Euros From the US

On PayPal's US business fee pages, receiving 100 EUR from a payer in another country costs 3.49% + 1.50% international + 0.39 EUR fixed = 5.38 EUR, leaving 94.62 EUR with no conversion. If PayPal converts the money at the listed 4% spread you keep about 90.84 EUR. Your own country's PayPal page may show different rates.

Fee = amount x 4.99% + 0.39 EUR (3.49% for a domestic payer). The conversion spread is taken from the amount left after the fee. PayPal lists 4% and 3% for different transaction types, or "another amount disclosed during the transaction". Standard card payments use 2.99% plus the international 1.50% by the same arithmetic (PayPal does not name cards separately for the international fee, so this is an assumption).

How the three costs stack

1) Percentage fee: 3.49% for PayPal balance, Venmo and invoice payments. 2) International add-on: 1.50% when the payer is in another country. 3) Fixed fee: 0.39 EUR for euro payments. Conversion is a fourth cost that only applies if money changes currency. On small payments the fixed fee matters more: on 10 EUR the fee is 0.89 EUR, which is 8.89%.

FAQ

How much does PayPal charge to receive euros from a US payer?

On PayPal's US business fee pages, a 100 EUR payment from another country costs 3.49% + 1.50% international + 0.39 EUR fixed = 5.38 EUR, so you receive 94.62 EUR if no currency conversion happens. If the payer's money must be converted, a 3% or 4% spread is taken from what remains. Your own country's page may differ.

Is the 1.5% international fee charged when a US client pays me in euros?

PayPal's page lists an additional 1.50% on commercial transactions received from another country. A client in the US paying a euro account falls under that wording on the US page, but check your country's page, which is the one that applies to you.

When does the currency conversion spread apply?

When PayPal converts money from one currency to another, for example the client pays in USD but your balance is in EUR. If the client pays in euros and you hold euros, there is no conversion on that step. PayPal may also disclose a different conversion amount at checkout.

Why is the fixed fee 0.39 EUR and not $0.49?

PayPal lists the fixed fee per currency: 0.49 USD, 0.39 EUR, 0.39 GBP, 0.59 CAD, 0.59 AUD. It is charged in the currency you receive.

How much should I invoice to receive exactly 100 EUR?

With the international fee and no conversion, invoice about 105.66 EUR. Use the target mode of the PayPal fee calculator for other amounts.
