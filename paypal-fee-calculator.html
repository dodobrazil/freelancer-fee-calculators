How it is calculated

PayPal charges a percentage plus a fixed fee that depends on the currency received. Payments from another country add 1.5%. If money is converted, a spread (3% or 4%) is taken from what remains.

Net = (payment x (1 - (rate + intl)%) - fixed) x (1 - conversion%).
What to invoice = (target / (1 - conversion%) + fixed) / (1 - (rate + intl)%).

Example: $100 at 3.49% + 1.5% + $0.49 leaves $94.52; a 4% conversion spread leaves about $90.74.

FAQ

What are PayPal's fees for receiving payments?

On PayPal's US business pages: 3.49% plus a fixed fee for PayPal payments (2.99% for standard cards), where the fixed fee depends on currency, e.g. $0.49 USD or 0.39 EUR.

Is there an extra fee for international payments?

PayPal lists an additional 1.50% on commercial transactions received internationally. Another PayPal page for some regions shows different figures, so check your country page.

How much does currency conversion cost?

PayPal's page lists a spread of 4.00% when paying/receiving in a different currency and 3.00% for other conversions, or another amount disclosed during the transaction.

How does it compare with Wise?

We only verified one comparison number: Wise lists receiving a USD wire/Swift payment at a fixed 6.11 USD, and domestic receiving as free. Wise's sending and conversion rates were not confirmed, so no full comparison is made.

How do I invoice so I net a specific amount?

Use 'I want to receive X' mode; the invoice amount covers percentage, fixed fee and conversion.
