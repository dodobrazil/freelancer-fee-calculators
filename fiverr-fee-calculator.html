How it is calculated

Fiverr's seller commission is a percentage of the order total (extras and tips included, per Fiverr's guides). The buyer separately pays a service fee on top of the price.

Seller net = order price x (1 - commission%).
What to charge = target net / (1 - commission%).
Buyer total = price + 5.5% of price + $3.50 if the order is under $200.

Example: a $100 gig at 20% nets $80; the buyer pays $100 + $5.50 + $3.50 = $109. To net $80 you charge $100.

FAQ

How much does Fiverr take from sellers?

Fiverr's Help Center says the freelancer gets 80%, i.e. a 20% commission, and its business guides state 20% on every sale including tips.

What does the buyer pay?

Fiverr's Help Center says a 5.5% service fee, plus a $3.50 fee on orders under $200. Some older pages mention $3 under $100.

Is the buyer fee taken out of my earnings?

No. The buyer fee is charged to the buyer on top of your price.

How do I price a gig to net a set amount?

Use 'I want to receive X' mode. Price = target / (1 - commission%).

Are withdrawal fees included?

No, they could not be verified from an official page. Check Fiverr's payments help page for your withdrawal method.
