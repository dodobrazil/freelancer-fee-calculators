Fiverr Withdrawal Fees: PayPal vs Payoneer vs Bank

Fiverr's own withdrawal fee is $0 to PayPal, $1 for a bank transfer via Payoneer and $3 to a Payoneer account. The cheapest on Fiverr's side is not always the cheapest overall, because PayPal and Payoneer can add their own conversion charges that Fiverr's page does not quantify.

How to choose

  • Small balance, USD, PayPal account already set up: PayPal has no Fiverr fee and the lowest minimum ($1).
  • Large withdrawal: a flat $1 or $3 is a tiny share, so the exchange rate you get matters far more than the fee. Compare the real rate for your currency before you withdraw.
  • Under $20: bank transfer via Payoneer is not available (minimum $20).
  • Plan for waits: the first withdrawal and any change of method add a 24-hour hold.

FAQ

What does Fiverr charge to withdraw earnings?

Fiverr's Help Center lists $0 for PayPal, $1 for a bank transfer via Payoneer and $3 for a Payoneer account. PayPal and Payoneer can add their own conversion or processing charges, which Fiverr does not quantify.

What are the minimum and maximum withdrawal amounts?

The minimums are $1 for PayPal, $10 for a Payoneer account and $20 for a bank transfer via Payoneer. The maximum is $5,000 per withdrawal transaction; you can make several withdrawals for larger amounts.

How long does a Fiverr withdrawal take?

PayPal takes about 24 hours, a Payoneer account up to 2 business days, and a bank transfer via Payoneer 1-3 business days in local currency or 5-7 for a USD wire.

Is there a waiting period?

Yes. Fiverr lists a 24-hour wait after your first withdrawal and after adding or changing a withdrawal method, and 48 hours after updating your verified phone number.
