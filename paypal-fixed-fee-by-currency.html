PayPal Fixed Fee Per Transaction by Currency (2026)

PayPal adds a fixed fee to the percentage on every commercial payment you receive, and the amount depends on the currency: 0.49 USD, 0.39 EUR, 0.39 GBP, 0.59 CAD, 0.59 AUD, up to 149 HUF or 49 JPY. On a $10 payment the 0.49 USD fixed fee alone is 4.9%.

Last checked: 2026-11-26 against PayPal's US business fees page, linked below. Anything we could not confirm is marked unverified.

Why it matters for small payments

A fixed fee hurts small invoices most. A 10 USD payment at 3.49% + 0.49 USD loses 0.84 USD (8.4%). A 100 USD payment loses 3.98 USD (3.98%). The same payment from another country adds another 1.50%.

FAQ

What is PayPal's fixed fee per transaction?

On PayPal's US business fees page, the fixed fee for commercial transactions depends on the currency received: 0.49 USD, 0.39 EUR, 0.39 GBP, 0.59 CAD and 0.59 AUD are among the 24 currencies listed. It is added to the percentage fee.

Is the fixed fee charged in addition to the percentage?

Yes. The page lists a percentage rate (for example 2.99% for standard cards or 3.49% for PayPal checkout) plus the fixed fee in the currency you receive. Payments from another country add 1.50% on top.

Does the fixed fee apply to Send/Receive Money for Goods and Services?

The page lists 2.99% for that option with no fixed fee shown. We read only the part of the page we fetched, so confirm on the page if you rely on it (unverified).

Are these fees the same in my country?

Not necessarily. These are from PayPal's US business page. Other country pages can show different amounts, so check your own country's PayPal fee page.
