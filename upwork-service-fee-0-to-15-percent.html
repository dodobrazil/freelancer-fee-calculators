Upwork Service Fee: 0 to 15%, and How to Find Your Rate

Upwork does not charge one flat percentage. The freelancer service fee ranges from 0% to 15% per contract, so the number that matters is the one on your own proposal or offer.

Last checked: 2026-11-24 against Upwork's help page, linked below. Anything we could not confirm is marked unverified.

What Upwork says

  • The fee is charged on your earnings and ranges from 0% to 15% per contract verified
  • It is set based on factors Upwork describes as supporting a balanced and competitive environment across types of work; the page does not list the formula verified
  • Once a proposal, offer or contract is sent, the fee is locked in and does not change, though it can differ between contracts verified
  • If your client is refunded, Upwork returns the service fee you paid on that amount verified
  • No regular discounts are offered; special promotions may appear verified
  • A default rate for everyone is not stated unverified

How to find your rate

  1. When you write a proposal, the fee percentage is shown before you send it.
  2. When a client sends you an offer, the percentage is shown on the offer.
  3. After a contract starts, see it in your contract details and in your transaction summaries.

Exceptions

According to the same page, some work is treated differently: Upwork Payroll and Any Hire contracts have no freelancer service fee, Direct Contracts (clients you brought to Upwork) carry a reduced fee, and Enterprise terms vary by agreement, with about 10% typical. Exact rates for these cases are in your contract.

The service fee is only part of the picture. Getting the money out costs extra: see Upwork fees for freelancers outside the US.

FAQ

What is the Upwork freelancer service fee?

Upwork's help page says it is the fee you pay on your Upwork earnings and that it ranges from 0% to 15% per contract. It is fixed once the contract begins.

How do I find my service fee rate?

The fee percentage is shown when a client sends you an offer or when you submit a proposal. You can also see it in your contract details and in your transaction summaries.

Is there a default 10% fee?

The Upwork page we read states only the 0-15% range and does not name a default (unverified). It mentions about 10% for Enterprise clients, but exact Enterprise terms depend on the agreement. Use the rate shown on your own proposal.

Does the fee change during a contract?

No. Upwork says that once a proposal, offer or contract is sent, the fee is locked in, although it can differ between contracts.

Do I get the fee back if the client is refunded?

Upwork says that if your client is refunded, by you or through payment protection, it returns the service fee you paid on that amount.

Does everyone pay the same fee?

No. Upwork lists exceptions: Upwork Payroll and Any Hire contracts carry no freelancer service fee, Direct Contracts have a reduced fee, and Enterprise terms can differ.

Clients pay a separate one-time fee per contract: Upwork contract initiation fee.
