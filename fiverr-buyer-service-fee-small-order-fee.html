Fiverr Buyer Service Fee and the $3.50 Small Order Fee

On Fiverr's Help Center, buyers pay a 5.5% service fee on every order, plus a $3.50 small order fee when the order is under $200. A $100 gig costs $109.00 at checkout; a $10 gig costs $14.05. A $199 order costs $213.45, which is more than a $200 order at $211.00.

Last checked: 2026-11-22. This page is about what the buyer pays. Rates are from Fiverr's Help Center, linked below. Each fee is marked verified or unverified.

What the buyer pays at checkout

Total = price + 5.5% service fee + $3.50 if the price is under $200. The 5.5% is calculated on the order price.

The $200 cliff

Because the $3.50 fee stops at $200, a $199 order costs $213.45 and a $200 order costs $211.00. A buyer about to order a $199 gig pays less by choosing a $200 package, and a seller pricing at $199 adds a fee their buyer sees. This follows from the listed rates; confirm at your own checkout.

Small gigs pay the most

On a $10 gig the buyer pays $14.05, an extra 40.5% over the price. At $100 it is 9%, and from $200 it is 5.5%. If you sell cheap gigs, bundling into fewer, larger orders lowers what your buyer pays in fees.

What this is not

The buyer fee does not come out of the seller's earnings. The seller's own commission (20%) is a different fee, verified on Fiverr's Help Center. For the seller side, enter your own rate in the Fiverr fee calculator.

Fiverr's Help Center and Payment Terms (Sept 2025) say $3.50 under $200. Some older pages say $3 under $100. We use the newer figure.

FAQ

What is the Fiverr buyer service fee?

Fiverr's Help Center lists a 5.5% service fee paid by the buyer on top of the order price. It is not deducted from the seller.

What is the Fiverr small order fee?

Fiverr's Help Center and Payment Terms (Sept 2025) list a $3.50 fee for orders under $200. Some older pages say $3 under $100, so check the amount shown at your checkout.

Why does a $199 order cost more than a $200 order?

The $3.50 fee applies only below $200. At $199 the buyer pays $199 + $10.95 + $3.50 = $213.45. At $200 the buyer pays $200 + $11.00 = $211.00. Using the listed rates, the cheaper total is the larger order.

Does the buyer fee reduce what the seller earns?

No. The buyer fee is paid on top of the price. The seller's commission is a separate fee (20%, per Fiverr's Help Center).

Is my fee the same?

Fees can change and may be shown differently at checkout. The amount on your Fiverr checkout page is the one that counts.
