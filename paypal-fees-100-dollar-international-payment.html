How Much Does PayPal Take From a $100 International Payment?

On PayPal's US business fee pages, a $100 PayPal payment from another country costs 3.49% + 1.50% international + $0.49 fixed = $5.48, so you receive $94.52 if no currency conversion happens. If PayPal also converts the money at the listed 4% spread, you receive about $90.74. Your own country's PayPal page may show different rates.

Last checked: 2026-11-22. Rates are from PayPal's US business pages, linked below. Anything we could not confirm is marked unverified.

The worked example

A client in another country pays you $100 for an invoice. Fee = 100 x (3.49% + 1.50%) + $0.49. Domestic, the same payment would cost $3.98 and leave $96.02, so the international surcharge adds $1.50.

Other currencies

The fixed fee changes with the currency. A 100 EUR payment (0.39 EUR fixed) at the same percentages leaves 94.62 EUR, or about 90.84 EUR if a 4% spread applies. Fixed fees: $0.49 USD, 0.39 EUR, 0.39 GBP, 0.59 CAD, 0.59 AUD.

FAQ

How much does PayPal take from a $100 international payment?

On PayPal's US business fee pages, a $100 PayPal payment from another country costs 3.49% + 1.50% international + $0.49 fixed = $5.48, so you receive $94.52 if no currency conversion happens. If PayPal also converts the money at the listed 4% spread, you receive about $90.74. Your own country's PayPal page may show different rates.

Is the international fee charged on top of the normal fee?

PayPal's page lists an additional 1.50% on commercial transactions received from another country, added to the domestic rate. Your country page may differ.

Does the fixed fee depend on the currency?

Yes. The fixed fee listed on PayPal's pages is $0.49 USD, 0.39 EUR, 0.39 GBP, 0.59 CAD and 0.59 AUD per transaction. A 100 EUR payment at the same percentages leaves 94.62 EUR; with a 4% conversion spread about 90.84.

How do I avoid the conversion spread?

The spread applies when money is converted. Receiving and holding the currency the client pays in avoids it on that step, but your bank may convert later at its own rate, which is not in this table. PayPal may also disclose a different conversion amount at checkout.

What if my own rate is different?

Type your own percentage and fixed fee into the PayPal fee calculator. If PayPal shows a different rate for your account or country, that figure is the one that counts.
