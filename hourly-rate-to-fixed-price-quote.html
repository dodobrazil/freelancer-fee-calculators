Hourly Rate to Fixed Price: How to Quote a Freelance Project

A fixed price is your hourly rate times the hours, plus room for revisions, plus a surcharge if the client is in a hurry, then raised so that the platform fee does not eat your target. Worked numbers below.

Last checked: 2026-11-27 against the official help pages linked below. Anything we could not confirm is marked unverified.

The formula

  1. Hours x rate = base.
  2. Base + revision buffer (percent of base) = subtotal.
  3. Subtotal + rush surcharge (percent of subtotal), if any = quote.
  4. Raise the quote so you keep your target after fees.

FAQ

How do I turn an hourly rate into a fixed price?

Multiply estimated hours by your rate, add a buffer for revisions, then a surcharge if the deadline is rushed. Example: 20 hours at $50 is $1,000; a 15% revision buffer makes $1,150.00; adding a 25% rush surcharge on top makes $1,437.50.

How big should the revision buffer be?

There is no official figure; 10% to 20% is a common rule of thumb, not a verified fact. Use your own history of how many revision rounds clients actually ask for.

Do platform fees come out of the fixed price?

Yes, on Fiverr and Upwork the freelancer's fee is deducted from what the client pays, so quote the price that leaves your target after fees. The table gives that price at published or example rates (Upwork's 10% is an example within its 0% to 15% range, not a confirmed default).
