The service fee comes first

Upwork deducts its freelancer service fee from each payment before you can withdraw. The fee varies per contract and is locked once it starts; a 0-15% range appears in an Upwork page excerpt, and the exact rate is on your proposal. We could not confirm a default rate (unverified). FAQ

What does Upwork charge freelancers outside the US to withdraw?

Upwork's help pages list $0.99 per withdrawal for Direct to local bank (non-US). Direct to U.S. bank is listed at $2.99 when your tax address is outside the US (from 2026-09-01, re-read on Upwork's page 2026-11-26), and a USD wire transfer at $50. Your own bank may add incoming fees or an exchange spread.

Which Upwork withdrawal method is cheapest?

On the published flat fees, direct to local bank ($0.99) is the lowest of the three we could verify. The table shows the fee as a share of the amount withdrawn. Withdrawal to PayPal is not included because we could not confirm its fee (unverified).

Is the Upwork service fee different outside the US?

Upwork does not state a country-specific service fee on the pages we checked. The freelancer service fee varies per contract (a 0-15% range appears in an Upwork page excerpt) and is shown on your proposal. We could not confirm a default rate (unverified).

Why is my bank balance lower than expected?

The receiving bank or currency conversion can take a further cut. Upwork notes your bank may add incoming fees; exchange spreads are set by your bank and are not in the table.
