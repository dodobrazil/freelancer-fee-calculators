PayPal Instant Transfer Fee (1.5%) Explained

Moving your PayPal balance to a bank account or debit card instantly costs 1.50% of the amount, with a minimum of 0.50 USD on US dollar withdrawals. A standard transfer has no fee when no currency conversion is involved. On $1,000 that is $15.00 for speed.

How to avoid it

  • Use a standard transfer if you can wait; it has no fee without conversion.
  • Keep currency conversion out of the transfer: a spread is a separate cost, see the conversion fee explained.

FAQ

How much is PayPal's instant transfer fee?

PayPal's US page lists 1.50% of the amount transferred for instant transfers to a bank account or debit card, with a minimum fee of 0.50 USD for US dollar withdrawals.

Is there a maximum instant transfer fee?

The part of the page we read does not state a maximum fee, so we cannot confirm one. It lists transfer limits instead: 25,000 USD per transaction to a bank and 50,000 USD per transaction to a card.

Is a standard transfer free?

The page says a standard transfer to a bank account or debit card has no fee when no currency conversion is involved. Conversion adds a separate spread.

Does the 1.5% come on top of the normal PayPal sales fee?

Yes. The sales fee is taken when you receive the payment. The instant transfer fee applies only when you move the balance out immediately.
