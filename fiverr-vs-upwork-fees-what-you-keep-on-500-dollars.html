FAQ

Is Fiverr or Upwork cheaper for a freelancer on a $500 job?

On published numbers, Fiverr takes 20% of the order (you keep $400.00 to PayPal). Upwork's freelancer service fee ranges from 0% to 15% per contract, so at its highest (15%) you keep $424.01 after a $0.99 local-bank withdrawal. Your actual Upwork rate is shown on your proposal.

What does the client pay on each?

On Fiverr the buyer pays a 5.5% service fee on top, so $527.50 for a $500 order (no small-order fee at $200 or more). On Upwork the client pays a one-time contract initiation fee of $0.99 to $14.99 per contract, which Upwork says varies; it is separate from your service fee.

Why do you not give one Upwork number?

Upwork states a 0% to 15% range and says the fee is fixed once a contract starts. We could not confirm a default rate (unverified), so the table shows 0%, 5%, 10% and 15% instead of guessing.
